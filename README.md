# School Medical Management System

## Introduction

**School Medical Management System** is a school health management software designed for a school's medical office. The system comprehensively supports the management of all healthcare activities, student health records, and facilitates connections between the school, parents, and medical staff to ensure optimal care for students.

## Key Features

- **Homepage Introduction**  
  - Provides information about the school, health-related materials, and a blog for experience sharing.

- **Student Health Record Management**  
  - Allows parents to declare student health records: allergies, chronic diseases, medical history, vision, hearing, vaccination status, etc.

- **Medicine Submission for Students**  
  - Parents can submit medication to the school for medical staff to administer to students.

- **Medical Event Management at School**  
  - Records and handles medical events such as accidents, fevers, falls, epidemics, etc.

- **Medicine and Medical Supplies Management**  
  - Tracks inventory and usage of medicines and supplies during the handling of medical events.

- **Vaccination Management at School**  
  - Sends vaccination consent forms to parents  
  - Prepares student vaccination lists  
  - Administers vaccinations, records results, and monitors post-vaccination status

- **Periodic Health Check Management**  
  - Sends health check notification forms and checklists to parents for confirmation  
  - Prepares lists of students for health checks  
  - Conducts checks, records results  
  - Sends results to parents and schedules individual consultations if abnormalities are found

- **User Profile and Health History Management**  
  - Tracks information and health history of students and users

- **Dashboard & Reports**  
  - Provides statistics and reports on student health and school medical activities

## Technologies Used

- **JavaScript** (56.4%) - Frontend
- **CSS** (27.4%) - User interface design
- **Java** (14.3%) - Backend logic, API
- **HTML** (1.8%) - Web page structure
- **Shell**, **Batchfile** - Automation, deployment support
